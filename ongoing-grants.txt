The PI holds two ongoing grants from the Swedish Research Council whose
periods partly overlap with this application.

The first is a VR research grant, "Differential Privacy with Accuracy"
(ending 2026), which developed DPella---a programming framework for
reasoning about privacy--accuracy trade-offs in differentially private
queries. That project focused on the theoretical foundations and language
design of DP systems. The current proposal builds directly on these
results in WP3, where DPella's accuracy techniques are adapted to an
agentic setting, but goes substantially beyond it by integrating DP as a
declassification primitive within an IFC system and targeting LLM-driven
query synthesis---neither of which is within scope of the expiring grant.

The second is a VR Special Call on Cybersecurity grant, "EDA: Towards
Enforcing Data Privacy Regulations" (started 2025), which applies a
combination of IFC and differential privacy to enforce GDPR principles in
data processing pipelines. That project targets regulatory compliance in
traditional software systems. The current proposal is complementary: it
targets AI agent pipelines specifically, introduces eBPF-based OS-level
enforcement, and addresses prompt injection and control-flow integrity---
none of which are within scope of the EDA project. The two grants share
conceptual foundations but address distinct problems with distinct methods.
